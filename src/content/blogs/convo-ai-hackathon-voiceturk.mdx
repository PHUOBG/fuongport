## Turning an Idea Into a Champion Product

Competing in the **CONVO AI Hackathon Danang University 2026** alongside my three teammates from **DSUC Blockchain Club** was an intense and rewarding engineering sprint. Our goal with **VoiceTurk** was to make AI-powered voice interaction natural, responsive, and genuinely useful for everyday communication.

## Balancing Engineering and Product Storytelling

Wearing two hats as **Software Developer** and **Marketing & Communications** taught me how closely technical execution and clear user storytelling are connected:

1. **Rapid Iteration:** Focusing on core end-to-end voice flows ensured our prototype stayed reliable during live demos.
2. **User-First Clarity:** Communicating why a feature matters is just as important as building the architecture behind it.
3. **Team Alignment:** Tight collaboration across our four-member team allowed us to move from concept to working demo and take home **Champion**.

Explore the codebase in the [VoiceTurk repository](https://github.com/Liinh-Git/VoiceTurk).
