## Bridging Computer Vision and Physical Hardware

Building **AgriHarvest AI** — an autonomous crop harvesting robot — pushed me to look far beyond notebook accuracy. As **AI Vision Lead** working alongside **1 Software** and **4 Hardware** teammates, every model prediction had to translate reliably into physical robotic action.

## Key Engineering Takeaways

- **Real-World Lighting & Variance:** Models that look crisp on clean validation splits need careful data and threshold tuning under real camera conditions.
- **Hardware-Software Contracts:** Defining clear coordinate and confidence interfaces between the vision pipeline and hardware controllers eliminated integration bottlenecks.
- **Iterative Testing:** Close feedback loops with the hardware sub-team helped us refine detection robustness and reach **Top 12 at AIoT Developer InnoWorks 2026**.
